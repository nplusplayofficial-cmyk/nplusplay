K3 · How to Play

1. Durations

Choose K3 1 Min, 3 Min, 5 Min or 10 Min. Each mode has its own local demo history and period stream.

2. Dice result

Three six-sided dice roll and reveal a generated virtual result. Sum, Big/Small and Even/Odd are derived from the three dice.

3. Categories

Total shows sums 3–18. 2 Same, 3 Same and Different expose the corresponding K3 selection groups and open the same betting sheet.

4. Betting

Select an option, choose amount, quantity and demo multiplier, agree to the rules and confirm while betting is open. The final 5 seconds are locked.

5. History, My Bets & Chart

All three are in-page WINGO-style tabs. History shows results, My Bets shows your demo selections, and Chart shows recent sum distribution.

6. Virtual-credit notice

This prototype uses virtual credits only. No real-money wagers, payouts, deposits, withdrawals or real-money commission settlement are processed.
