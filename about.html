About N+ PLAY

This page is a prototype disclosure for the current virtual-credit demo. It is not a substitute for a formally reviewed privacy notice, terms of service, or legal risk disclosure before a public release.

Confidentiality & account data

Account data is used to support sign-in, account identification, virtual-wallet records, referrals and member features. Access should be limited to the signed-in account and authorized administrators for their stated tasks. Never share your password, email OTP, recovery details, or session tokens.

Public-facing privacy terms, data-retention periods, support contact details, and data deletion procedures must be reviewed and published before production launch.

Risk disclosure

VIP points, gift-code values, attendance rewards, referrals and the virtual safe in this prototype are simulated credits. They have no cash value and do not earn interest. No real-money deposits or cash withdrawals are implemented by these pages.

Game activity, odds, outcomes, or rewards must not be described as guaranteed income. The rebate section remains disabled until server-validated game settlement and audit records are complete.

Referral & reward rules

Each account may attach one referral code. Self-referrals are rejected. The single-level demo reward is credited once only after a qualifying virtual-credit request of at least 200 points is approved by an administrator. Admin approval and reward settlement are server-side events.

Referral codes should not be used to mislead or pressure others. Multi-level betting commissions are not part of this N+ demo implementation.

Security status

  • Use a unique strong password and protect email access.
  • Review wallet changes in the ledger and report unexpected activity through the configured support channel.
  • Do not trust messages asking for passwords, OTPs, bank details or transfers.
  • Before deploying the backend migration, validate it in local staging and restore-test backups.
