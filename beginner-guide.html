Beginner’s guide

N+ PLAY's member features currently operate in virtual demo-credit mode. The steps below do not describe a cash deposit or a way to earn guaranteed income.

1Create your account

Open the sign-up screen, enter your details and verify the email using the one-time code. Choose a strong unique password and never share the code with anyone.

Open sign-up ↗

2Use your own account

Open Account to review your profile and virtual wallet balance. Only your own wallet and ledger should be visible to your signed-in account.

Open account ↗

3Invite a friend

Open the VIP Centre and copy your individual referral code or link. The invited account must attach the code during sign-up. Self-referrals and multiple referral attachments are rejected.

Open invitation centre ↗

4How referral rewards work

The referring member receives 20 virtual points only after the invited account's qualifying virtual-credit request of at least 200 points is approved by an administrator. The award is one-time and recorded in the server ledger.

5Gift codes and attendance

Gift codes can be redeemed once per signed-in account. Attendance can be claimed once daily, and an approved virtual-credit request is required for the attendance bonus in this demo.

Gift codes ↗ · Attendance ↗

6Wallet and safe

The safe page only moves virtual points between two demo balances. It does not calculate interest or support a cash withdrawal.

Open virtual safe ↗

7Review records

Use the wallet activity report to see transactions recorded for your account. It should not be treated as a bank statement, proof of profit or a complete game-wise wagering report.

Open activity statistics ↗

8Account and support

Keep your login secure, sign out on shared devices and use official N+ navigation. Never share passwords or verification codes in messages.
