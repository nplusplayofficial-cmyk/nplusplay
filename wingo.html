WINGO · How to Play

1. Game overview

WINGO is a round-based virtual-credit game experience. Each round has a fixed duration and one generated result.

2. Durations & periods

Choose 30 SEC, 1 MIN, 3 MIN or 5 MIN. Each duration has its own period sequence and history context.

3. Timer & betting cutoff

Selections can be confirmed while the round is open. New confirmations are locked during the final 5 seconds. When the timer reaches zero, the round closes and the result is revealed.

4. Number & payout

Select any number from 0 to 9. If the result exactly matches your selected number, the demo return is contract amount ×9.

5. Colour & payout

Green: 1,3,7,9 = ×2; 5 = ×1.5. Red: 2,4,6,8 = ×2; 0 = ×1.5. Violet: 0 or 5 = ×4.5.

6. Big / Small & payout

Big = 5–9 and Small = 0–4. A correct Big or Small selection returns contract amount ×2.

7. Amount, quantity & multiplier

Total stake = amount × quantity × multiplier. For settlement, the contract amount is 98% of the total stake, matching the displayed 2% service-fee example.

8. Confirmation

You must agree to the rules. The system checks the current round and prevents confirmation after the cutoff or when demo credits are insufficient.

9. Result & settlement

After a round ends, confirmed demo selections are compared with the generated result and marked WON or LOST. The result popup remains for about 3 seconds.

10. History & My Bets

Each duration keeps its own recent results and demo selections. Up to 500 recent rounds are retained locally for the prototype.

11. Charts & statistics

Charts show historical demo results only. Previous patterns do not guarantee or predict future results.

12. Sync & reconnect

The interface uses clock-based round boundaries and restores stored prototype state after reload. A production deployment should use a server-authoritative game engine.

13. Virtual-credit notice

This prototype uses virtual credits only. It does not process real-money wagers, payouts, deposits, withdrawals or real-money commission settlement.
